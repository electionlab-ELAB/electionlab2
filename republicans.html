About the Tracker

How ElectionLab calculates the primary average

ElectionLab combines published national primary polls using a weighted average. Newer surveys receive more influence than older surveys, while larger sample sizes and higher-quality pollsters also receive additional weight.

Candidate averages are recalculated automatically whenever a published poll is added through the ElectionLab admin portal.
