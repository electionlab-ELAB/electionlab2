# ElectionLab 2.0 — Portrait credits & usage

The site references these publicly hosted Wikimedia Commons photographs. They are loaded at runtime (not bundled into the ZIP). The public website renders them in black and white / feathered cutout style. They are editorial illustrations; **no appearance indicates an endorsement or 2028 nomination**.

* **JD Vance** — Official Vice Presidential Portrait, January 2025, by Daniel Torok, public domain. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:January_2025_Official_Vice_Presidential_Portrait_of_JD_Vance.jpg
* **Gavin Newsom** — *Gavin Newsom 2025.jpg*, Maximilian Steyger, Open Government Licence v3.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gavin_Newsom_2025.jpg (image filtered and feathered). Licence: https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/
* **Donald Trump** — *January 2025 Official Presidential Portrait of Donald J. Trump (Transparent Version).png*, Daniel Torok, public domain. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:January_2025_Official_Presidential_Portrait_of_Donald_J._Trump_(Transparent_Version).png

Candidate photography entered through the admin roster uses each candidate's `photo_url`. You are responsible for rights and attribution of any further photos you add. The admin field accepts publicly accessible HTTPS photo URLs.
