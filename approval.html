About the Average

How ElectionLab calculates approval

ElectionLab gives more weight to newer polls, larger samples, likely-voter surveys, and higher-quality polling organizations. Older surveys gradually lose influence as newer data becomes available.

The tracker currently includes national approval polling only. State-by-state approval polling is not included.
